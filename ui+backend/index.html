How It Works

This tool uses Natural Language Processing and Machine Learning to classify online news articles as real or fake, based on linguistic patterns and source credibility.

Our AI model has been trained on thousands of verified news articles to identify common patterns in misinformation. Simply paste any news headline or article, and receive an instant authenticity assessment with a confidence score.
